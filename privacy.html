MarvikOne Posts - Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026.

MarvikOne Posts is a private tool used only by the MarvikOne content channel (GTA 6 news and videos) to upload its own videos to its own TikTok account. It is not offered to the public.

Data we access

Only data of the MarvikOne TikTok account that authorizes the app: basic profile information (open ID, display name, avatar) and the permission to upload videos to that account. We do not access data of any other TikTok user.

How we use it

Solely to send videos created by MarvikOne to the MarvikOne TikTok account. We do not sell, rent or share any data with third parties, and we do not use it for advertising.

Storage and security

Access tokens are stored encrypted on the channel owner's computer (operating system credential vault). They are never published or sent to third parties.

Retention and deletion

Data is kept only while the authorization is active. Access can be revoked at any time in the TikTok app: Settings and privacy > Security > Manage app permissions. After revocation, stored tokens are deleted.
